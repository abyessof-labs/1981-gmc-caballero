# SAAQ mechanical inspection — the clauses that apply to this car

Extracted from the official **SAAQ Road Vehicle Mechanical Inspection Guide** (update August 2024, ISBN 978-2-550-92253-7), which is the standard this car is judged against for out-of-province registration.

Guide PDF: https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/mechanical-inspection-guide.pdf

## How defects actually work

| Type | Consequence (Highway Safety Code) |
|---|---|
| **Minor defect** | s.531 — owner must repair within 48 hours. *"At the expiry of that period, no person may put the vehicle back into operation"* until repairs are proven. |
| **Major defect** | s.354 — no operation at all until repairs are proven. |

**The 48 hours is a grace period for continuing to drive, not a repair deadline.** Blowing it carries no penalty — the car simply stops being legal to drive until fixed and verified.

**It almost certainly never applies here.** That clock targets vehicles already in service (roadside check, periodic inspection). This car will be trailered, stored unplated, and presented to the inspection as a *gate to registration*. A failure just means no registration yet — fix on your own schedule, return for re-inspection.

**Practical consequence:** do all repairs *before* booking the inspection. Not because of a clock, but because every failed inspection costs another fee and another trailer trip.

> **Ask SAAQ:** the re-inspection procedure and fee, and whether repairs must be verified at the same authorized centre. That's the detail that affects the timeline.

## ⚠ The gotcha that can waste a whole trip

From General Information:

> An inspection agent **must refuse to inspect** a vehicle where *"dirt or other obstructing material (ice, grease, rust, etc.) prevents a complete visual inspection of all vehicle components."* The vehicle must be unloaded.

**Degrease and clean the undercarriage before presenting it**, or risk being turned away and paying for the slot twice. Sequence: clean underside → repairs → inspection → registration.

## Clause-by-clause, against this car's known issues

### Structure and rust

| Clause | Wording | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| **3.1(a)** side rails | *"broken, cracked, bent, **perforated by rust**, or shows a sign of repair or a modification that weakens the structure"* | Frame rails |
| **3.1(b)** cross members | same wording | Frame cross members |
| **3.1(c)** parts of frame used to fix the body | *"missing, inoperative, not securely mounted, damaged, cracked, broken or bent"* | **The rotted middle body mount** |
| **3.1(e)** underbody | structural member *"cracked, broken, bent, perforated by rust, improperly assembled or shows a sign of repair… that weakens the structure"* | Floor structure |
| **11.6** passenger compartment floor | *"warped, cracked or perforated"*; and if *"so perforated that it constitutes a hazard for passengers by reason of a lack of solidity"* → **major** | Floor pans, and the smuggler's box floor (accessed from inside the cab on a '78–87 car) |
| **11.7** load space | floor or side *"does not prevent the load from falling out"* | Bed floor |

**Note the "sign of repair that weakens the structure" language.** A previous amateur patch fails on its own merits. This matters both for the replaced passenger floor (verify how it was welded in) and for how the body mount repair gets done.

### Brakes

| Clause | Wording | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| **5.2c** | Master cylinder *"is not securely mounted or **shows signs of oozing**"* | Corroded master cylinder |
| **5.2.1** | *"With or without brake application, there is a leak of brake fluid along the system other than oozing"* | Disclosed brake line |
| **5.1(d)** | Parking brake cable *"weakened (frayed), seized, missing, disconnected, not securely attached or broken"* | The "emergency brake" item |

The guide's own definition: ***"Oozing can be detected when a component is damp, whereas a leak leaves a drop of liquid or a trace on the ground."*** **Damp is enough to fail.**

> The orange crust on the master cylinder in the purchase photos sits at the joint where it meets the **brake booster** — the position of a rear-seal weep. Budget for master cylinder *and possibly booster*, not "one brake line."

### Exhaust

| Clause | Wording | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| **7.1** general | a component *"intended by the manufacturer is missing, not securely mounted, or has a leakage of gas"* | Corroded manifolds/crossover |
| **7.1** s.92 | ***"Flammable material is leaking on a component of the exhaust system"*** | **The oil dripping onto the manifold** (the bluish cold-start smoke) |
| **7.1(a)** | *"The muffler, the catalyzer or the resonator is missing or not securely mounted"* | See [`emissions-and-exhaust.md`](emissions-and-exhaust.md) — **not applicable if the car left the factory without a cat** |
| **7.1** | leakage of exhaust gases *"under the passenger compartment where the floor is perforated"* | Rusty exhaust + any floor perforation compounds |

The oil-on-manifold leak is a **named defect**, not a tidy-up-later item. Trace and fix the source (valve cover gasket is the usual suspect) before the inspection.

### Glazing

**8.1(a)** — the windshield is defective if:
- *"tarnished, cloudy or broken in a way that reduces the driver's vision of the road or road signs"*
- there is *"an intersection of cracks or missing flakes more than 12 mm (½") in diameter that meet on the area covered by the wipers"* (excluding under the mirror and a 75 mm strip top and bottom)
- *"loss of transparency in the area covered by the wipers, or exceeding 10% of the total surface"*

### Accessories — easy to overlook, likely to bite on a 45-year-old car

| Clause | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **9.2** Horn | Must work; audible at ~60 m (200 ft); control easy to reach |
| **9.3** Wipers **and washers** | Wipers must work at specified stroke rates, blades make even contact — **and the washer system must work.** A dead washer pump fails. |
| **9.4** Heater/defroster | Blower must work, adequate air to windshield, *"a system is inadequate if it does not produce any heat"* — **and heating liquid piping inside the passenger compartment must not be cut, cracked, worn or leaking** (heater core) |
| **9.5** Neutral safety switch | With brake depressed, the starter must **not** activate in any position other than P or N |
| **9.6** Speedometer/odometer | Must work |

> **9.5 connects to a known issue.** The unhooked shift indicator cable was priced as a $0–150 cosmetic item — but a maladjusted or disconnected shift linkage is exactly what throws the neutral safety switch out of adjustment. **Test the crank-in-gear check and the indicator cable together.**

### Lights

| Clause | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **1(j)** Backup light | *"does not switch on, or is not white"*; where gearshift-activated, must switch off out of reverse |
| **1(k)** Dashboard lights | *"One of the lights in the dashboard does not switch on"* — **every** dash light must illuminate. With loose wiring in the bay and the radio hanging out, expect burnt bulbs. |

### Body and restraints

| Clause | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **11.4** Doors | Must open, close and engage properly from inside and outside; hinges not missing, cracked, broken or seized |
| **11.8** Seat belts | Belts, buckles, retractors and **anchorages** must work and be unaltered; ***"a seat belt anchorage is not securely mounted"*** is a defect |
| **11.9** Seats | Securely fixed on original rails/anchorages; adjustable seats must lock in the chosen position |
| **11.3** Bumpers | Manufacturer-provided bumpers or supports *"missing or of a different size or material than intended"* |

> **11.8 is the second defect from the body mount issue** — the reinforcement carries the driver's lower belt anchor. See [`body-mount-repair.md`](body-mount-repair.md).
>
> **11.9** — the interior photos show exposed bracketry between the buckets. Confirm both seats are properly mounted and lock.

### Tires and wheels

| Clause | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **10.1** | A tire *"touches a fixed part of the vehicle"* is a defect; tires with exposed cord fail |
| **10.2** | Wheel studs, bolts, nuts or fasteners *"damaged, repaired by welds, or of the wrong size"* |

> Relevant with the aftermarket wheels. Also confirm nobody notched or bent the rear frame to clear them — that would trip 3.1 as a *modification that weakens the structure*. Check DOT date codes; raised-white-letter radials on a stored car are often ancient.

## What is NOT inspected

- **Emissions.** Quebec runs no emissions test for light passenger vehicles. The CCC/ECM/AIR-pump question is irrelevant to the inspection — **except** via the exhaust clause above, which names the catalyzer as an exhaust component.
- **Section 6.2 "Engine control system"** covers the accelerator pedal, linkage and return springs — throttle mechanics, not emissions computers.

## Pre-inspection checklist derived from the above

- [ ] Body mount reinforcement repaired and seat belt anchor verified solid
- [ ] Brake line(s) + master cylinder (check booster) — no damp, no weep
- [ ] Parking/emergency brake cable working
- [ ] Oil leak onto exhaust manifold traced and fixed
- [ ] Windshield replaced
- [ ] Exhaust gas-tight, muffler and resonator secure
- [ ] Wipers work at both speeds; **washer pump works**
- [ ] Heater produces heat, blower works, no heater core leak
- [ ] Crank-in-gear test passes (P/N only); shift indicator aligned
- [ ] Backup lights work; **every** dash light illuminates; horn works
- [ ] Both doors open/close/latch cleanly; driver's door gap even
- [ ] Seat belts: buckles, retractors, anchorages all sound
- [ ] Seats securely mounted and locking
- [ ] Tires: no exposed cord, no rubbing, DOT dates checked
- [ ] **Undercarriage degreased and clean enough to be inspected**
