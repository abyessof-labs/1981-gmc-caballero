# Parts sourcing — Canada

Where to buy for this car, and the identification traps that make the wrong part arrive.
Researched September 2026. Prices are list at time of writing, not quotes.

## Rule zero: shop it as an El Camino

Almost nothing is catalogued under "GMC Caballero." The 1978–87 Caballero is the badge-engineered
twin of the Chevrolet El Camino on GM's A/G platform, and the interior is shared. Search
**"1978-87 El Camino"** (and for some trim, "Malibu"). Vendors who do list the Caballero — Auto
Metal Direct, OER, Mill Supply — file it under the El Camino part number anyway.

Corollary: a listing that says "El Camino" and nothing about GMC is still almost certainly correct
for this car. A listing that says *Caballero Laredo/Diablo* is trim-specific and probably is not,
since this is a base **Z88**.

## Door panels — the specific job

Relates to [`known-issues.md`](known-issues.md) → Interior → *"Driver door armrest pad and base both
missing; panel board torn around the footprint."*

### How the door trim is built

The 78–87 door has **two separate pieces**, sold separately:

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Upper door panel** | Padded vinyl over a board, with a metal top rail, inner window felt, door lock ferrules, and the chrome mylar "hockey stick" trim strip |
| **Lower door panel** | A plainer board, vinyl- or carpet-faced depending on trim |

### The trap: 1981 is a break year

**78–80 uppers and 81–87 uppers are different parts and do not interchange.** The 1978–80 panels
carry dielectric (heat-welded) seam lines; the 1981–87 panels are smooth without them. This is the
single most common mis-order on this body.

PUI Interiors encodes it in the part number prefix:

- `78AD**-P` → 1978–1980 — **wrong for this car**
- `81AD**-P` → 1981–1987 — **correct for this car**

### PUI pre-assembled upper panels, 1981–87

Sold as a **pair** (LH + RH front). There is no single-side option, which matters here because only
the driver's side is damaged.

| Colour | Part no. | Approx. list (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `81AD70-P` | ~$493 |
| Navy blue | `81AD18-P` | ~$470 |
| Maroon | `81AD01-P` | ~$460 |
| Camel 48 | `81AD48-P` | — |

Interior colour for this car is **not yet confirmed** — settle it from the SPID sticker (still
unlocated, see known-issues) or a paint-code/trim-tag read before ordering.

### Lower panels — check the speaker cutout

Lowers are catalogued **with a single speaker opening** and **without**. They are not
interchangeable in appearance. Confirm what the driver's door actually has before ordering.
Lower door panel *carpet* is a separate part again (also sold as a G-body pair, 1978–88).

### Fitment expectation

Owner reports on El Camino and G-body forums are consistent: PUI panels look correct — the grain
and trim are the best available — but fit has degraded. Expect to trial-fit repeatedly, re-drill or
elongate screw holes, and re-seat the mylar shield. Budget the afternoon, not the hour.

### Before ordering anything

Photograph the driver's door with the trim in place and confirm **which of the two boards carries
the armrest footprint** that is torn. If the damage is confined to the lower board, a pair of uppers
at ~$500 USD is the wrong purchase.

## Sources, ranked for a car in Montréal

### Tier 1 — Canadian stock, no brokerage

| Vendor | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Chevelle Depot** — chevelledepot.ca | Canada | Already the supplier for the window felt set (WFK 1411 78). **Authorized PUI dealer** with a dedicated PUI brand page. Their catalogue currently surfaces the *78–80* upper (`78AD70-P`) — ask them to bring in **`81AD70-P`**. Best first call: existing relationship, prices in CAD, no brokerage, no surtax question. |
| **Muscle Cars & Classics** — musclecarsandclassics.ca | Windsor, ON | Ships from Canada, explicitly no brokerage. Carries PUI. Second quote. |
| **Canadian Classic Auto Supply** — autoholic.ca | Canada | Authorized Auto Metal Direct distributor. More use for the rocker and quarter-panel rust than for trim. |

### Tier 2 — US specialists (best selection, worst landed cost)

- **Dixie Restoration Depot** — the 78–88 G-body specialist. Carries door panels, door panel
  components, arm rests, and trim panels for these exact years, **new *and* used** — which matters,
  because base-trim pieces are often only available second-hand.
- **OPGI**, **Ecklers / Classic Chevy (El Camino Store)**, **OER Parts**, **GBodyParts.com**,
  **Summit Racing**, **1A Auto** (sells an Accu-Form 2-piece set listed for El Camino *and*
  Caballero).

### Tier 3 — used and salvage, close to home

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| **ARPAC / piecesvertes.com** | The Quebec auto recyclers' association. ~35 member yards on one searchable inventory, updated daily, with a parts-request function. A 1981 is a long shot, but the search is free and covers the whole province at once. |
| **Auto Reno** — autoreno.com | Used parts, **Vaudreuil** — effectively next door to Île-Perrot. Worth a phone call. |
| **LKQ Québec** — lkqcanada.ca | Runs a dedicated antique/classic parts page. |
| **Kijiji / Marketplace** | Standing saved searches on "El Camino", "Caballero", and "Malibu 1981" — parts cars and stripped interiors surface regularly, and this is how the car itself was found. |

### Tier 4 — the swap meet (time-sensitive)

**Barrie Automotive Flea Market — September 10–13, 2026**, Burl's Creek Event Grounds, Oro Station,
Ontario. Canada's largest automotive flea market, 600+ acres. Roughly 6½ hours from Île-Perrot.

For used G-body interior trim this is the single densest source in the country, and it runs **six
days from this writing**. If the panels are wanted cheap and original rather than new and
reproduction, this is the trip to make.

### Tier 5 — don't buy, recover

For a base-trim driver-quality car with one bad door, recovering the existing board is very often
the better value than a $500 USD pair of panels plus shipping and brokerage.

| Shop | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Automobilia Inc** | 1242 boul Marcel-Laurin, Saint-Laurent · 514-342-8400 | Convertible tops, custom interiors, general upholstery, auto glass. 25+ years. States they can supply factory-fit interiors **complete with door panels**, or fabricate. ~30 min from Île-Perrot. Also has auto glass in-house — relevant to the cracked windshield. |
| **Fibrenew** (Montréal Est / West Montréal) | Mobile | Vinyl, plastic and leather repair — specifically door panels and armrests. The right call if the goal is repairing the tear around the armrest footprint rather than replacing the board. |

## Import cost note (as of September 2026)

Buying from a Canadian vendor sidesteps this section entirely, which is most of why Tier 1 is Tier 1.

For US orders:

- Canada has held a **25% counter-tariff on US motor vehicles** since April 2025, and a **further
  counter-tariff list takes effect September 8, 2026**.
- CUSMA-originating parts generally clear at **0%**, but that depends on the vendor supplying a
  valid certification of origin — which a small consumer parts order typically does not come with.
- Whether reproduction interior trim is captured by the September 8 list is **unconfirmed** —
  the Finance Canada product list could not be retrieved during this research. Verify against
  canada.ca before placing a large US order, or simply buy domestic.
- Courier brokerage fees apply on top regardless of duty, and are the usual reason a $500 part
  lands at $700.
