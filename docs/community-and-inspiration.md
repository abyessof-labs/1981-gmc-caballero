# Community and build inspiration

Forums worth an account, and the threads/articles worth reading before deciding what this car
should become.

> **How this was assembled.** The forum domains below are blocked by this session's network egress
> policy, so the threads were located through web search rather than opened and read page by page.
> Titles, URLs, and post counts are as reported by search; treat the per-thread descriptions as a
> pointer, not a summary. Everything here should open normally in a browser.

## Forums — where to sign up

Ranked for *this* car: a 1981 G-body Caballero, base Z88 trim, LG4 305 4-bbl, THM200C/250C.

### 1. GBodyForum — sign up here first

https://gbodyforum.com/ · Caballero/Camino subforum: https://gbodyforum.com/forums/chevrolet-el-camino-gmc-caballero.7/

The 1978–1988 A/G-body community, and the only place where "Caballero" is a first-class citizen
rather than a footnote. The subforum is literally named **Chevrolet El Camino / GMC Caballero** and
covers the GMC trim packages (Diablo, Amarillo, Laredo, Royal Knight) alongside the Chevy ones.
There is a separate **G-Body Build Threads** section.

Why it matters for us specifically: everything on our open list — rust in the G-body cowl and
bed corners, window felt fitment, THM200C vs 250C identification, LG4 Q-jet and CCC-era emissions
plumbing, SPID decoding — is G-body-common, not El-Camino-specific. The answers here are about the
platform.

### 2. El Camino Central — sign up second, for the archive

https://www.elcaminocentral.com/ · Forum index: https://www.elcaminocentral.com/forums/

~47,000 members and 940,000+ posts, running since 2002. The largest El Camino community by a wide
margin, and 24 years of archived threads means most questions have already been answered — search
before posting. Chevy-centric, but Caballero owners are regulars and the cars are mechanically
identical; there are long-running threads specifically on what differs between the two badges
(https://www.elcaminocentral.com/threads/differences-between-camino-and-caballero.32778/) and on
the A-body/G-body split at 1981/1982
(https://www.elcaminocentral.com/threads/78-81-a-body-82-87-g-body.246795/) — relevant, since a
1981 sits on the *last* year of the A-body designation for this shell.

It also hosts the NECOA subforum: https://www.elcaminocentral.com/forums/national-el-camino-owners-association.86/

### 3. NECOA — National El Camino Owners Association

Facebook group: https://www.facebook.com/groups/NECOA/ · Region pages e.g. https://www.facebook.com/NECOA14/

A club rather than a forum — incorporated in Nevada in 2012, organised into numbered regional
chapters, and it describes itself as covering "the Chevrolet El Camino **and her sister car made by
GMC**," which is unusually explicit about Caballeros. Reported membership is in the 25,000–30,000
range (that number is self-reported and probably counts the Facebook group). Worth joining for
regional events and for the parts-sourcing network; not the place for long technical threads.

### 4. Team Chevelle

https://www.chevelles.com/ · El Camino subforum: https://www.chevelles.com/forums/el-camino.535/

Deepest knowledge base for Chevelle-family chassis and drivetrain, with a dedicated El Camino
subforum that carries G-body threads. Secondary for us, but strong when a question is really about
GM A/G chassis, rear axles, or small-block V8s rather than about the pickup body.

### Practical sign-up notes

- **Post an introduction with photos** in the model subforum before asking anything. Response rate
  on a thread with pictures is dramatically higher than one without.
- **Start a build thread early**, even at "just bought it, here is the rust." A build thread is the
  single most effective way to get free diagnosis over months — people follow it and volunteer.
- Most of these boards gate classifieds and private messages behind a small post count. Sign up now
  so the account is aged before there is a part you need.
- Our open questions map cleanly onto first posts: VIN check-digit anomaly, SPID sticker reading,
  pan-bolt transmission ID. Each is a good, specific, answerable thread.

## Where this car could go

Three directions the reference material above splits into, for a black base-trim 1981 with the
LG4 305:

1. **Clean survivor.** Fix the rust, keep the drivetrain, keep the base trim honest. References:
   the 1981 Amarillo on Barn Finds, the Curbside Classic car, the survivor threads on GBodyForum.
   Cheapest path, and base black is a genuinely good-looking spec that does not need help.
2. **Driver restomod.** Body sorted, then modern brakes, suspension, and either a rebuilt/tuned 305
   or an LS. References: the 1983 5.3-swap thread, the Streetside LS car, the Holley catalogue.
   The single most common outcome in these communities.
3. **Trim tribute.** Base Z88 dressed toward Diablo or Amarillo. Cheap visually, but the forums are
   opinionated about it — read the Diablo identification threads first.

No decision needed now; this is a reading list, not a plan.
