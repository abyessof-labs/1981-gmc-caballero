# Wheels and tires — sizes for a raked, white-letter look

How to get the look of the reference photo (below) on this car: black five-spoke mags with polished
lips, a slightly bigger rear tire, and **raised white letter (RWL)** tires. Covers what the car has
now, which RWL tires exist in which sizes, what fits under a 1978–87 El Camino/Caballero, and
three size packages.

> **Sourcing note.** Tire sizes and USD prices were read directly from Coker Tire product pages and
> one National Tire & Wheel listing (Oct 2026). Fitment comes from El Camino Central and
> GBodyForum owner reports, located by search. CAD prices are as reported by search, not opened.
> Confirm fitment by test-fitting one wheel and tire before buying a set.

## The reference look

The owner supplied a side-profile photo of a red Caballero as the target. Its wheels are **the same
style the car has now**: black five-spoke mags with polished lips, fat sidewalls, and a slightly
taller tire at the rear.

**Sizes measured from that photo.** I scaled the photo to the 117" wheelbase, so these figures are
**inferred ±0.5"**, not read from the tires:

| | Front | Rear |
|---|---|---|
| Tire overall diameter | ~26.0" | ~26.7" |
| Rim lip diameter | ~16.5" | ~16.4" |
| Likely wheel diameter | **15"** | **15"** |
| Likely tire | ~235/60R15 | ~245–255/60R15 |

The fat sidewall is what makes this look, and it needs a 15" wheel. On a 17" wheel the tires drop
to 40–45 series and the look changes.

**Do first: read the size off the current wheels and tires.** The wheel size (for example `15X7`)
and usually the brand are cast on the back of a spoke or inside the barrel. The tire size is on the
sidewall. With those two numbers every option below becomes an exact comparison. The wheel brand is
**unidentified**. It is not a Vision 141 Legend 5 (which has twisted spokes) and not a Weld
Draglite (which has round holes).

## Fitment limits, 1978–87 El Camino / Caballero

| | |
|---|---|
| Bolt pattern | 5 × 4.75" (5 × 120.65 mm) |
| Stock tire | P205/75R14, **26.1"** tall. The speedometer is calibrated to this. |
| Up to 8" wide | Fits with about 4" backspacing, no rubbing at stock height. 4" is the stock G-body backspacing. |
| Front, 15×7 | 235/60R15 can rub slightly at full left lock. 4¼" backspacing has been reported to bring the rim lip close to the upper control arm. |
| Rear, 15×8 | 4" backspacing leaves about 1/8" between the tire and the wheel-opening lip. 275/60R15 is the **largest rear tire that fits without major work**. It needs 4–4¾" backspacing and may rub in hard cornering on stock suspension. |

Sources: El Camino Central's
[’78–’87 tire/wheel fitment guide](https://www.elcaminocentral.com/threads/78-87-tire-wheel-fitment-guide-read-before-buying.42907/),
[Anyone running 275/60/15?](https://www.elcaminocentral.com/threads/anyone-running-275-60-15.139705/),
and GBodyForum's [what is the biggest tire](https://gbodyforum.com/threads/what-is-the-biggest-tire.16593/).

## Raised white letter tires — what exists

RWL tires are almost all **15"**. Above 15" the choice is very thin.

| Tire | RWL sizes that matter here | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **BFGoodrich Radial T/A** | 215/65R15, 225/60R15, 235/60R15, **255/60R15**, **275/60R15**, 295/50R15, plus 70-series 15s. In 16" there is only 245/50R16. | The classic choice; S-rated (112 mph). Coker lists 235/60R15 at **USD $212** and 275/60R15 at **USD $245**. Search reported Canadian prices for 275/60R15 from about **CAD $228–$454** ([Tires and Co](https://www.tiresandco.ca/car-tires/bf-goodrich/radial-t-a/275-60-r15-107-s-rwl.html), [Tire Direct](https://tiredirect.ca/products/radial-t-a-61789), [Camaro Depot](https://www.camarodepot.ca/bfgoodrich-radial-t-a-white-letter-275-60r15-6308000)). Owners report the white letters going brown; Coker warns some tire shines cause it. |
| **Cooper Cobra Radial G/T** | 215/65R15, 235/60R15, 245/60R15, 255/60R15, 275/60R15, 295/50R15 | 14" and 15" only. Usually cheaper than the T/A. |
| **Mickey Thompson Sportsman S/T** | 215/70R15, 225/70R15, 235/60R15, 275/60R15 | 15" with RWL. |
| **Coker Pro-Trac Street Pro III** | 17": **225/45ZR17, 245/45ZR17, 255/45ZR17, 275/40ZR17**; also 18–20" | The only factory-built RWL in modern low-profile sizes. W-rated. 255/45ZR17 is **USD $191** at Coker. |
| Glue-on rubber letters | Any size | A way to put white letters on any tire. Durability unverified. |

## Size packages

Tire heights are calculated from the size code. Speedometer error is against the stock 26.1" tire,
because the rear tire drives the speedometer.

### A. Same look, slightly bigger rear — **recommended**

| | Wheel | Tire (RWL) | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | **15×7**, about 3¾–4" backspacing | **235/60R15** | 26.1" |
| Rear | **15×8**, about 4–4½" backspacing | **255/60R15** | 27.0" |

This is closest to the photo, with the rear about ⅓" taller than in the photo. The rear tire is
about 10 mm wider. It fits at stock height with no reported rubbing. The speedometer reads about
**3% slow**: it shows 100 km/h at about 103. Both the T/A and the Cobra come in both sizes.

### B. Classic big-and-little rake

| | Wheel | Tire (RWL) | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | **15×7** | **225/60R15** or 235/60R15 | 25.6" / 26.1" |
| Rear | **15×8**, 4–4¾" backspacing | **275/60R15** | 28.0" |

This is the most common G-body look, with an obvious rake and a fat rear. The rear fills the wheel
opening and **may rub in hard cornering** on stock suspension. The speedometer reads about **7%
slow**, so it shows 100 km/h at about 107. To correct that, fit a different speedometer driven gear
in the transmission tailshaft; the gear depends on which transmission the car has (THM200C or
250C), which is still unconfirmed. The T/A, Cobra and Sportsman S/T all make 275/60R15. For a 225/60R15 front, use the T/A.

### C. Bigger wheel, modern profile

| | Wheel | Tire (RWL) | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | **17×8** | Pro-Trac Street Pro III **245/45ZR17** | 25.7" |
| Rear | **17×9** | Pro-Trac Street Pro III **275/40ZR17** or 255/45ZR17 | 25.7" / 26.0" |

This gives bigger mags, but at the cost of the photo's look. The sidewalls get thin, the rake from
tire height disappears, and the only RWL source is Coker. Check backspacing on a 17×9 against the
1/8" rear clearance above before buying.

16" is a dead end for RWL: BFG's only 16" Radial T/A RWL is 245/50R16, which is *shorter* than
stock at 25.6".

## Decision state

| Question | State |
|---|---|
| Current wheel brand and size | **Open.** Read the back of a spoke. |
| Current tire size | **Open.** Read the sidewall. |
| Package A, B or C | **Leaning A.** The owner asked for a "slightly bigger rear tire" with white letters on similar mags. |
| Keep the current wheels and add a wider rear pair, or buy a new set | **Open.** Depends on what the current wheels are. |
| Speedometer gear for a taller rear | **Open.** Only needed for package B; first needs the transmission ID. |
| Timing | After the rust and body-off repair (see [`body-off-frame.md`](body-off-frame.md)) |
