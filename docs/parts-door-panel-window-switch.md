# Door panel & window switch — clips and retainers

Fastener list for pulling and reinstalling the front door trim panels, with the power window
switch hardware called out separately. Everything here is the 1978–87 GM A/G-body platform, which
is shared across El Camino / Caballero / Malibu / Monte Carlo — vendors catalogue almost all of it
under "El Camino," and the Caballero is the same door.

**Two things to confirm on the car before ordering:**

1. **Power or manual windows.** A base Caballero (Z88) is not automatically power-window equipped —
   `A31` is an option. If a door has a crank, skip section B entirely and use the crank clip in
   section D. The glove-box SPID sticker (still unread, see `known-issues.md`) settles it.
2. **Model year of the switch bezel.** 1979 used a one-year-only *metal* switch bezel; 1980 onward
   is the vacuum-metallized chrome *plastic* "stepped" bezel. Repro switch/retainer kits are
   commonly listed as 1981–87 or 1979–87 — a 1980 car takes the plastic-bezel parts, but eyeball
   the existing bezel before buying. This car is a **1981**, so it is squarely in the common range.

## A. Door trim panel → door shell

| Part | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Door trim panel retainer, white nylon barbed stud | **Auveco 11796** (OE GM **3065575**) | 7/8" head, 5/16" stem, 3/4" stem length, triple-head. 1978-up GM. Sold 50/box. Cross-refs: Dorman 45466 / 45681, NAPA 6651107 |
| Same clip, sold in small quantity | OPGI **L240679** (each) · Dixie Restoration Depot **DM01330** (set of 20) | Same white nylon barbed-stud clip, restoration-vendor packaging |
| Sealed variant (retainer with sealer washer) | Auveco **20616** | Use if the existing clips have the foam/butyl sealing washer — keeps water out of the clip hole |
| Door pull-strap screws | Dixie **DM00109** | Trim screws, chrome flange washer |
| Door pull-strap end cap / insert (hides the strap screws) | SS396 **IPS-254** · end cap **532490** · chrome bezel base **764370** · 1981–87 brushed-aluminum insert (Eckler's/Classic Chevy 55-199374) | These snap covers are brittle and usually break on removal — order before you start |
| Armrest base + pad | (driver side both **missing** on this car — see `known-issues.md`) | Reproduction armrest sets: Dixie **DM00588** family |

**Quantity:** budget **10–12 retainers per door**, buy 20. They are technically reusable, but a
40-year-old panel that has been off before will have broken or missing ones and the panel board is
already torn around the driver's armrest footprint.

## B. Power window switch (this is the specific ask)

| Part | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Power window switch retainer clip** | **GM 9722397** — black spring steel | The clip that snaps the switch/bezel into the door panel. **2 clips per switch bezel.** GM also lists it as "Retainer, Dr Lock Switch" — the door lock switch uses the same clip |
| Same clip, aftermarket | Classic Industries **9722397** (repro) · OPGI **L240078** · OER · Dorman **49272** (2-pack) · GBodyParts (fits GM 1976–2002) | |
| Switch, 1-button (passenger door) | GM **20043940** — chrome-plated plastic, square corners, door-panel mount. Repro: The Parts Place **EL10874Z** | |
| Switch, 2-button LH (driver door) | GM **20061549** · OPGI **L240075** · SS396 **GWS-017** · Eckler's 55-198618 | |
| Switch pigtail / connector | Dorman **645-942**, or a generic 5-way 1980s GM power-window pigtail repair kit | Only if the connector body is crumbling. A small speed-nut style retainer with a plastic cover holds the connector onto the switch — save it |

**Quantity for this car (2 doors):**

- driver 2-button switch — 2 clips
- passenger 1-button switch — 2 clips
- door lock switch, if equipped — 2 clips

Order **6–8 clips**. They cost pennies and are the part that gets lost in the door cavity.

**Worth knowing:** complete reproduction switches (Eckler's / Chevelle Depot / SS396) ship
**assembled with the chrome stepped bezel and the black spring-steel clips already installed**. If
the existing bezel is pitted or the switch is intermittent, buying the complete switch is often
cheaper than buying bezel and clips separately.

## C. Order at the same time — you will destroy these getting the panel off

| Part | Number | Why |

## Sourcing notes

- **Canada:** Chevelle Depot (chevelledepot.ca) ships domestically — worth pricing against US
  vendors once duty and brokerage are counted.
- **Fastener-only US sources:** Auveco direct, Fastener Zone, AutoFastenersAndClips, DealerShop —
  these sell the Auveco boxes cheaply if the 50/100-count quantity is acceptable.
- **Restoration vendors** (small quantities, correct-for-the-car packaging): OPGI, Dixie
  Restoration Depot, SS396 / Ground Up, Eckler's / El Camino Store, Classic Industries, OER,
  GBodyParts, Chicago Muscle Car Parts.
- The authoritative fastener callouts are in the **1981 Chevrolet/GMC Chassis & Body service
  manual**, section on door trim panel removal — worth having before the panel comes off.
