# Parts sources

The Caballero is GMC's badge-engineered Chevrolet El Camino on GM's A/G-body platform. **Nearly all sheet metal, interior, trim and mechanical parts interchange** — always search **"El Camino"** as well as "Caballero", and **"78-88 G-body"** for structural parts. The parts count under "El Camino" is far larger.

> **Platform naming:** 1978–81 was the **A-body**; the identical platform was renamed **G-body** for 1982–88. This car is a late A-body, but everything is cross-listed as "78-88 G-body." Searching "A-body" alone mostly returns 1964–72 Chevelle content.

Vendor-specific parts already priced for this car are in the Parts table in [`reference-links.md`](reference-links.md). This file is the broader sourcing map.

## Weatherstripping

Full kits fitted for 78-87 El Camino/Caballero:

- [Ecklers full kit — roof rails, door seals, window felts](https://www.ecklers.com/1978-1987-el-camino-weatherstrip-kit-roof-rails-door-seals-and-window-felts-55-849656-1.html) — most complete single kit
- [1A Auto / Fairchild door & roof rail kit](https://www.1aauto.com/1978-87-chevrolet-el-camino-gmc-caballero-door-and-roof-rail-weatherstrip-seal-kit-fairchild-automotive-1awsk00104/i/1awsk00104) — name-brand repro
- [Ecklers door window felt set](https://www.ecklers.com/1978-1987-el-camino-door-window-felt-set-55-198440-1.html)
- Canadian: **Muscle Cars & Classics** and **Chevelle Depot** carry the same lines

**Installation:** [1981 El Camino & G-Body Weatherstrips (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iyVll4D9JqA) — same year and body · [Ecklers upper window seal install PDF](https://dalhems.com/f/d/fb0753f1456c44f7b6f3a5fe00971e67/ECK02-0133.pdf) · [Ecklers tech guide](https://www.ecklers.com/installing-new-weatherstripping-tech-guide.html) · [Steele Rubber tips](https://blog.steelerubber.com/2018/08/30/auto-weatherstrip-installation-tips/) · [Muscle Road door seal guide](https://muscleroad.com/blogs/news/how-to-install-an-on-body-door-weatherstrip-seal-a-general-guide) and [window felt / run channel guide](https://muscleroad.com/blogs/news/how-to-install-door-window-felt-glass-run-channel-weatherstrip-kits-a-general-guide)

Method: strip old seal, scrape all old adhesive from the channel, dry-fit the whole perimeter loose, then work it in **2–3 ft sections starting at the lowest point** where water collects, applying weatherstrip adhesive as you go. Painter's tape holds curved sections while the glue grabs. Retainer pins must line up with factory holes — gentle stretching may be needed.
