## Post-purchase inspection — owner assessment

Recorded after taking delivery. **This supersedes the photo-era assessment below where they conflict.**

- **Mechanically sound and runs very well**, with one exception: the **emergency/parking brake**.
- **Almost no rust** overall — the photo-era rust concerns were largely not borne out.
- **The entire passenger floor is new** (replaced by a previous owner).
- **One major defect:** the **middle driver's side body mount** — the reinforcement above it has rotted away; the mount is attached at the bottom only and no longer attached to the body. **This is the item that will not pass safety.**
- **Canadian-market car confirmed** — bought new 1981, registered in Ontario, one owner until 2025. See [`emissions-and-exhaust.md`](emissions-and-exhaust.md).

The photo-era entries below are kept as the record of what was documented at purchase. Where the owner assessment now contradicts them, they are marked **needs re-verification** rather than closed — physical confirmation should replace a general impression before anything is struck.
